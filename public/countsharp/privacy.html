Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

CountSharp is published by Inzite Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the CountSharp app handles your information.

Summary

CountSharp does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device so it is there next time you open it:

  • Your settings, such as table rules and your chosen counting system
  • Sessions you log, including any casino names, results and notes you enter
  • Your training drill results

This information never leaves your devices. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Apple Watch and Siri

If you use the CountSharp Apple Watch app, the current shoe, your counting system and your basic strategy chart are sent directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. They are not sent over the internet or to us.

If you use CountSharp with Siri or Shortcuts, the app answers on your iPhone using the information stored there. Siri requests themselves are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-in
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind
  • We do not use third-party services that collect data
  • The app does not need an internet connection and does not send data over the network

Children

CountSharp is not intended for children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email support@inzite.com.
