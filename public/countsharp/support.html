Support

CountSharp is a blackjack card counting trainer and strategy companion for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact us

For help, bug reports or feature requests, email support@inzite.com. We aim to reply within two working days.

It helps if you tell us your device model, your iOS version and the app version (shown in the App Store).

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. CountSharp works completely offline and does not need an account.

Where is my data stored?

Your settings, logged sessions and drill results are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them. See the privacy policy for details.

Which counting systems are supported?

Hi-Lo, Hi-Opt I, Hi-Opt II, Omega II, Zen Count, Wong Halves, KO, Red 7 and Ace-Five. You can change the system in Settings.

Can I match the rules at my table?

Yes. In Settings you can set the number of decks, whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17, doubling and splitting rules, surrender, blackjack payout and deck penetration. Basic strategy and deviations update to match.

Is this a gambling app?

No. CountSharp is for learning and practice. It does not take bets, connect to casinos or handle money.
