A deckbuilder with the pressure left in

Your hand carries over

Unplayed cards stay in your hand between turns. Ballast cards reward holding on: they trigger at the start of every turn they sit in your hand, until you spend them for a burst.

Wind-ups

Play a Wind-up onto your spring track and it gains tension every turn. Release it early for a quick hit, or let it wind to the limit and resolve at full force.

Ten heroes with their own stories

Riveter, Tidewitch, Salvager, Lamplighter and six more to unlock. Each has a backstory, a map ability, two combat abilities and a starting deck built around a different way to dive.

Six terrains, thirteen bosses

Every fathom rolls two of six drowned terrains, from the Flooded Foundry to the Lightless Trench, each with its own monsters and guardians. On Abyss difficulty, the Heart Engine itself waits at the bottom.

Boons and shrines

After each boss, choose one of three boons drawn from the terrains you just dived. Six shrines offer one-time bargains with real costs attached.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements through Game Center on iPhone and iPad, plus an optional account for cross-device sync.

Plays offline

Every run, setting and achievement is stored on your device. No connection needed to dive; online features are optional extras.

Short runs, deep decisions

Three difficulties, seeded runs for sharing, a bestiary and a card compendium that fill in as you play.

Four divers are ready at the dock. The other six unlock by playing: trigger Ballast cards, release a wind-up at high tension, bring down the Kelp Regent and more.
