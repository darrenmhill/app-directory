RealmQuest 2.0

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

RealmQuest 2.0 is published by Inzite Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the RealmQuest 2.0 app handles your information.

Summary

RealmQuest 2.0 is playable fully offline and needs no account. Your runs, settings, unlocks and achievements are stored on your device. The app contains no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you choose to send through the optional features described below.

On iPhone and iPad

The iOS app has no login or account system. Its online features are:

  • Game Center (optional): if you sign in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are reported to Apple. Game Center is provided by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy; the app does not receive your Apple ID and we do not store any Game Center data on our servers.
  • Global leaderboard: when a run ends, the app submits your score, hero, difficulty, fathom reached and the date to our leaderboard server, together with the player name you chose (by default your Game Center display name). The entry holds nothing else: no account, device or advertising identifier links it to you. If the device is offline, the score waits on the device and is sent later.
  • Bug reports: the "Report a bug" button opens your email app with a pre-filled message to support@inzite.com. Nothing is sent unless you send the email, and we only receive what you choose to include.

In App Store terms this is Gameplay Content, not linked to you, used only for app functionality, and never for tracking.

On the web and Android

These versions offer an optional account for syncing progress between devices. If you create one we store a username, a display name, a hashed password (never the password itself), your synced progress, settings and saved game, and any bug reports you submit from within the app (the text you type plus your username). You can delete the account and everything stored with it at any time in Settings → Delete Account.

Server logs

Our leaderboard server sees your IP address while handling a request and uses it only to limit abusive request rates. It is not stored with your scores or account.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with third parties
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera

Deleting your data

Everything stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app. Leaderboard entries submitted from iOS are anonymous; if you want one removed, email us the player name, score and date and we will delete it. Web and Android accounts can be deleted in Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes the account, scores, saved games and synced progress.

Children

RealmQuest 2.0 is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only personal information it can hold is the player name and, outside iOS, the optional account details you choose to enter.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email support@inzite.com or see the support page.
