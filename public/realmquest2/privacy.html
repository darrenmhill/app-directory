RealmQuest 2.0

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

RealmQuest 2.0 is published by Inzite Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the RealmQuest 2.0 app handles your information.

Summary

RealmQuest 2.0 is playable fully offline. Your runs, settings and achievements are stored on your device. The online features below are optional.

What we store if you use online features

  • Leaderboard scores: the player name you choose, your score, class, fathom reached and date.
  • Accounts (optional): a username, display name and a hashed password (we never store your password itself), plus your synced progress, settings and saved game.
  • Bug reports you choose to send: the text you type, plus your account username.

We use your IP address only briefly, to limit abusive request rates. It isn't stored with your account.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad you can sign in to Game Center to use leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is provided by Apple: your Game Center identity, scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app does not receive your Apple ID or any Game Center data beyond what is needed to report scores and achievements, and we do not store Game Center data on our servers.

What we don't do

  • No ads
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
  • No analytics SDKs

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, scores, saved games and synced progress. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Children

RealmQuest 2.0 does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only personal information it can store is the player name and optional account details you choose to enter.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email support@inzite.com or see the support page.
