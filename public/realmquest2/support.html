RealmQuest 2.0

Support

RealmQuest 2.0 is a roguelike deckbuilder set in Brassmere, a drowned clockwork city, for iPhone, iPad and the web.

Report a bug

The quickest way is from inside the game: tap the bug report button on the title screen and describe what happened. On iPhone and iPad this opens an email to us; on the web you need to be signed in so we can follow up. If you can, include the run seed shown on the victory or defeat screen; it lets us replay the exact dungeon you saw.

Contact us

For anything else, including feature requests and account questions, email support@inzite.com. We aim to reply within two working days.

It helps if you tell us your device model, your iOS version and the app version (shown in the App Store).

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. RealmQuest 2.0 plays fully offline. On iPhone and iPad there is no account at all: Game Center (optional) handles achievements and leaderboards. On the web, an optional account adds cross-device sync and in-game bug reports.

Where is my progress stored?

Runs, settings, achievements, the bestiary and the card compendium are stored on your device. On iPhone and iPad, achievements and leaderboards go through Game Center; on the web, signing in also syncs your progress to your account. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

On the web, open Settings → Delete Account in the game. This permanently removes your account, scores, saved games and synced progress. The iPhone and iPad app has no account; deleting the app removes everything it stored. To remove a global leaderboard entry, email us the player name, score and date.

What do the coloured words on cards mean?

Each damage type has its own colour and symbol: 🔨 Impact, ♨️ Steam, 🌊 Tide, ⚡ Spark, 🦀 Rust and 🎯 Pierce. Tap any card to read what each type does; monsters can resist or be weak to them.

How do I unlock the other heroes?

Four heroes are available from the start. The rest unlock through play: each locked hero on the title screen shows exactly what it needs, for example triggering Ballast effects a number of times or defeating a particular guardian.

What is a Wind-up?

Playing a Wind-up puts it on your spring track at tension 0. Each of your turns it gains tension. You can release it early with the Release button, or let it reach its maximum and release automatically; its effect multiplies by the tension at release.

What does Ballast mean on a card?

A Ballast card triggers its held effect at the start of each of your turns while it stays in your hand. You can still play it normally for its main effect.
